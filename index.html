FAQ

Is there a free plan?

Yes. You can try Recraft for free. Free-plan images are publicly visible and not licensed for commercial use.

Can I use my images commercially?

On paid plans, you get full ownership and commercial rights for images generated while subscribed — and you keep them after canceling.

Can Recraft create vector (SVG-style) graphics?

Yes. Recraft generates complex, editable vector graphics and can vectorize existing images.

How do custom styles work?

Upload a few of your images and Recraft turns them into a reusable, editable style — no training required.

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
